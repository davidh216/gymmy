Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Logging, plans, recovery and progress all work offline. An account is only needed for gym leaderboards and cloud backup.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, cloud backup, leaderboard entries and videos.

How are lift leaderboards ranked?

Bench, squat, deadlift and overhead press boards rank three ways: total (weight × reps), max weight and bodyweight %. Rows marked "Baseline" are reference sets to beat, not real people.

How do I report an entry?

Open the entry and tap Report. Entries that break the standards or are inappropriate are hidden and reviewed.
