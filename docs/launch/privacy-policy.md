# Gymmy Privacy Policy

_Draft for review. Last updated: [date]. Contact: [support email]._

Gymmy is a workout tracker with gym leaderboards. This policy explains what Gymmy keeps, where it goes, and how to delete it. In short: your training stays on your phone unless you create an account, nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

## Without an account

Everything stays on your iPhone: your profile (name, weekly goal, units), workouts, check-ins, custom exercises and plans, companions, gems and milestones. Deleting the app deletes it.

## With an account (optional)

You sign up with an email address and password, and pick a public username. Gymmy then backs up and syncs to your account:

- workouts you log in Gymmy, daily check-ins (sleep hours, soreness, energy, stress, rest days), custom exercises and plans, companions, milestones, XP, gems and settings.

It is stored with Supabase (our database provider) and only you can read it.

**Not backed up:** workouts imported from Apple Health, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and body weight read from Apple Health. Those stay on your phone.

> ⚠️ Decision needed before launch: check-ins are backed up, and their sleep hours can be prefilled from Apple Health. Either keep this and say so here, or stop syncing Health-prefilled sleep. See the "Health data in the cloud" decision.

## Gym leaderboards

If you post an attempt to a gym board, other people can see your username, companion, result, the video, the gym, the date and, for pound-for-pound boards, the bodyweight you entered. Global boards are visible to everyone. You can report entries and block people; reports are reviewed by Gymmy's moderators. When video moderation is switched on, new videos are scanned automatically by Hive (thehive.ai) for unsafe content before they go live.

## Apple Health

With your permission Gymmy reads sleep, workouts, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and body weight, and saves the workouts you log. Health data is used only for your readiness score, recovery and workout history inside the app. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

## Location

If you allow it, Gymmy uses your approximate location once, when you look for gyms nearby. The coordinates are sent to Gymmy's gym search (which looks them up in OpenStreetMap and keeps the results for a week for that ~1 km area, without anything that identifies you). Your location is not stored with your account or tracked in the background.

## Anonymous usage statistics

Gymmy records which screens and features get used (for example "finished a workout", "started a plan"), with a random install ID that isn't linked to your account, name or email, and rounded numbers only. Never your workouts, health data, name or email. Turn it off any time in Profile → Privacy.

## Crash reports

When crash reporting is on, app crashes are sent to Sentry with the error, device model and OS version so we can fix them. They don't include your name, email or health data.

## Deleting your data

- **Account and cloud data:** Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account, cloud backup, leaderboard entries, videos, reports and blocks.
- **Data on your phone:** Profile → Reset all data deletes everything stored locally; deleting the app does too.
- **Export:** Profile → Export gives you your workouts and check-ins as CSV, or everything as JSON.

## Children

Gymmy isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if anything changes, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
